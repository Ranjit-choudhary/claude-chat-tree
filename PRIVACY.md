# Chat Tree for Claude: privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Chat Tree is a browser extension that organises your own claude.ai chat list. It has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

## What it reads
- Your chat list on claude.ai (titles, summaries, dates, starred state, project names), requested directly from claude.ai using the session you are already signed into.
- Only if you turn on "Deep index" in Settings: the first message of each chat, cut to 600 characters.

## Where it goes
Nowhere. Everything is processed inside your browser. The optional smart grouping uses an AI model that ships inside the extension and runs on your computer. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

## What is stored
In the extension's local storage on your device: the cached chat list, your tags, your settings, the deep index (if on) and the smart-grouping results. Clear them from the extension's Settings, or by removing the extension.

## Sharing and selling
Your data is never sold, shared or used for anything other than showing you your own chats.

## Not affiliated
Chat Tree is an independent project, not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Anthropic.
